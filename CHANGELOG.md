# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)

First release as a package. Derived from the companion code archived at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23477614, whose outputs it reproduces on the STAID demonstration data.

- Configurable bed (`BedConfig`) and isotherms (Toth, Langmuir, Dubinin-Astakhov).
- Training with or without state-of-charge labels, latent initial state, observer.
- Metrics, exchanged energy, heat power, conformal prediction intervals.
- Examples: STAID store, laboratory silica gel bed. Tests and continuous integration.
